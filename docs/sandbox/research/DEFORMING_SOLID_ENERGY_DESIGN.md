# Deforming wet-solid energy: minimal implementation contract

Design only, 2026-09-07. No source/test changes, EOS runs or material admission. The next slice is a prescribed, quasi-static, porous-skeleton specimen with actual solid/fluid caloric closure and separately defined mechanical energy. It extends beyond a gas chamber, but does not predict free sludge sintering.

## Existing interfaces and the required separation

The actual solid caloric implementation is `incompressible_solid.py` (`SolidShomateCaloric`, `IncompressibleSolidPhase`), not `solid_caloric.py`. It supplies molar thermal/formation energy `u=h_standard(T)-p_reference*v_s` and `h=u+p*v_s`, with fixed intrinsic solid molar volume. Neither a skeleton modulus nor a recoverable strain energy is present. `SolidFluidStorage` explicitly reports `fixed_bulk_incompressible_solid_and_fluid_thermal_no_strain_or_interface_energy`; it closes actual liquid/gas pressure using `Vp=Vbulk-sum(Ns*v_s)`. `geometry.ReferenceSlab.deform` supplies compatible diagonal stretches/area/width/volume, not stress or a constitutive law. `SolidFluidHeat` decodes once and uses that thermal state for all shared faces. Existing `Rates.cell_power_w` is quadrature-integrated into `StepLedger.cell_work_j`; it does not distinguish multiple power components.

Do not reinterpret an old thermal `ConservedState` as a new total-energy state implicitly. Introduce an explicit identity-bearing initial-state constructor and a host/state energy scope `thermal_plus_skeleton_recoverable_plus_declared_interface_internal_energy`. At every stage retain:

- `Etotal [J] = Uthermal(N,T,Vbulk) + Eelastic(F) + Einterface(F)` per cell.
- The unchanged chemical formation-energy reference inside `Uthermal`. No second formation/latent-heat offset.
- An explicit energy-zero/reference deformation and reference interface area. Absolute interface energy is included; if subtracting a reference constant instead, the identity and initializer must consistently record that convention.

First implementation restricts solid inventories to fixed values and declares no solid reaction/solid transport. Gas/liquid transport and existing water phase transfer may change fluid inventories. This avoids silently ignoring derivatives of mechanical energy and intrinsic solid volume with respect to reacting solid inventories. Extension to reactions requires these derivatives and a material-evolution law, not a zero default.

## Chosen mechanically consistent narrow model

Use prescribed positive diagonal `F=diag(lambda_n,lambda_t,lambda_t)` from the motion snapshot, reference bulk `V0`, `J=det(F)`, and actual `V=J*V0`. Constant intrinsic solid volume and no closed pores imply `Vp=V-Vs`, hence **at fixed solid inventory** `dVp/dt=dV/dt`. This equality is derived for this contract, not assumed for general bricks. Porous skeleton bulk deformation can coexist with incompressible constituent grains: it represents rearrangement/deformation of the pore-containing skeleton, not affine compression of each solid grain.

Define a temperature-independent recoverable potential and an explicit interface internal-energy law. For the first manufactured provider only, a diagonal logarithmic-strain potential is convenient:

`ell_i=ln(lambda_i); theta=sum(ell_i); dev_i=ell_i-theta/3`

`Eelastic=V0*(K*theta^2/2 + G*sum(dev_i^2))`.

`K,G [Pa]` are positive explicit manufactured inputs over a bounded stretch domain. This is a chosen mathematical test constitutive model, not an inferred sludge modulus, globally stable finite-strain law or a quotation of the small-strain MOOSE model. Recoverable first-Piola stress follows the exact derivative `P_el=(1/V0)*partial Eelastic/partial F`. All diagonal derivatives must be analytic and independently checked. No claim of full three-dimensional shear/rotation support: the admitted motion is diagonal in a fixed material frame.

An optional nonzero **declared effective internal interface** test law is `Aint=Aint0*lambda_t^2`, `Einterface=gamma*Aint`, with explicit `Aint0 [m2]`, `gamma [J/m2]` and interface identity. This is a prescribed oriented-area constitutive fixture, not the slab heat-transfer face area, not measured grain area, and not a predicted surface-coarsening law. Its force is derived from the same energy. A reference area may not be obtained from external slab area without a material model. For wet use this effective skeleton/interface contribution must explicitly exclude liquid-vapor meniscus energy already associated with a capillary closure; the first model retains the existing zero-curvature/common-pressure fluid approximation and supplies no capillary pressure. A real wetting-dependent interface requires surface identities, occupancy/contact-angle law and distinct phase pressures before admission.

For a temperature-dependent Helmholtz surface energy, `gamma(T)*A` is generally **free energy**, not internal energy: its interface internal energy includes `A*(gamma-T*d_gamma/dT)` under the specified area state. Skeleton free energy has the analogous temperature derivative. The first provider therefore requires temperature-independent potentials, with no thermal eigenstrain or thermoelastic entropy. This restriction preserves the existing positive thermal heat-capacity proof and makes subtraction in the temperature inverse honest. Unknown entropy/capillary terms cannot be silently set to zero for a real material.

Optional manufactured viscous resistance can be admitted with dissipation power `D=eta*V0*sum(ell_dot_i^2) >= 0 [W]`, `eta [Pa s] >= 0`. Its Rayleigh dissipation potential is `Phi=D/2`; differentiation gives `P_visc=(1/V0)*partial Phi/partial Fdot`, hence `P_visc_i=eta*ell_dot_i/lambda_i`. Then `V0*P_visc:Fdot=D`. It is a constitutive fixture over a declared rate domain, not sintering viscosity. Omitting viscosity must be an explicit reversible-model selection, not an estimated zero coefficient.

With tension-positive stresses the complete first-Piola stress is

`Ptot = (1/V0)*partial(Eelastic+Einterface)/partial F + Pvisc - (p/V0)*partial Vp/partial F`.

Since `partial Vp/partial F=V0*J*F^(-T)` in this fixed-solid-volume contract, `sigma_tot=(Ptot*F^T)/J = sigma_skeleton+sigma_interface+sigma_visc-p*I`. Thus the coefficient of pore pressure is one **as a consequence of this incompressible-grain pore-volume model**. It is not an arbitrary measured Biot coefficient. Finite grain compressibility, closed-pore conversion or a different porosity law changes the derivative and requires a new thermodynamic provider.

The external quasistatic mechanical input is

`Wtot=V0*Ptot:Fdot = dEelastic/dt+dEinterface/dt+D-p*dVp/dt`.

Evolve total energy once: `dEtotal/dt = face_heat_and_enthalpy_net + Wtot + explicit_body_heat`. Consequently the implied thermal equation is `dUthermal/dt=face_net + D - p*dVp/dt + explicit_body_heat`. **Do not also add `-p*Vdot` or `D` as another source when Wtot already enters total energy.** Transported fluid enthalpy already includes flow work; no additional `p*Q` belongs on the same shared stream. Pressure work involves the actual liquid/gas common total pressure, not water vapor partial pressure.

The prescribed motion requires actuators that supply this complete constitutive stress, including deviatoric and surface contributions. A single imposed atmospheric pressure is not generally compatible. Each cell is a quasistatic representative specimen with declared actuator work; stress jumps between specimens require actuator/partition forces. Do not claim a force-balanced continuous brick or cancel unequal internal actuator work. Kinetic energy is outside this explicitly quasistatic model, with no dynamic validity inferred from small solver steps.

## Concrete next files and runtime interfaces

1. New `skeleton_energy.py`: frozen `DiagonalSkeletonEnergy` (explicit provider id/version/classification/source ids/assets; solid identity and fixed inventory signature; reference geometry; stretch/rate domain; energy reference; numerical bounds). `evaluate(motion_cell, fixed_solid_inventory)` returns immutable `SkeletonEnergyState`: `elastic_energy_j`, `interface_energy_j`, `elastic_piola_pa`, `interface_piola_pa`, `viscous_piola_pa`, `elastic_rate_w`, `interface_rate_w`, `dissipation_w`, energy/stress numerical bounds and full identity. The first concrete fixture implements the equations above; unknown real material parameters reject. Source classification never upgrades because a manufacturing test passes.
2. New `deforming_solid_storage.py`: `evaluate_at_temperature(T,N,motion)` rebuilds a current-volume `SolidFluidStorage` through its normal constructor and evaluates the original water/gas EOS. Return the original `SolidFluidState` plus mechanical energies, `Etotal`, identities and error budgets. `temperature_from_total_energy(E,N,motion,bracket,policy)` subtracts the known temperature-independent mechanical energy with explicit subtraction/parameter error and roundoff, then calls the existing thermal inverse with an honestly propagated target uncertainty. The old inverse currently accepts a scalar target, **not target uncertainty**: either extend it explicitly with tests or use a new bounded total-energy bisection that calls forward storage and adds all error bounds. Do not pass a rounded difference and drop its uncertainty.
3. New `deforming_solid_fluid_heat.py`: exact admitted host type and total-energy scope. One total-energy decode per stage; reuse a factored shared-face evaluator accepting real decoded states and current geometry, rather than passing total E to the old thermal decoder. Align reference count/area/every width/volume as in the approved gas design. Current solid volume, liquid saturation, liquid h/v, gas density, reaction-volume conventions and all half-cell distances must refer to the same motion snapshot. Initially reject solid reactions; wrap dynamic boundaries and water phase transfer only through explicit host support and preserved diagnostics.
4. Introduce accepted-stage component work records: elastic/interface storage rates, viscous power, pore work, total constitutive work, and any body heat. Apply **the same actual RK weights as Rates**; rejected trials must not enter the ledger. `cell_work_j` remains the authoritative sum, with the new component sum checked against it. Endpoint elastic/interface changes are an independent quadrature convergence check, not a replacement ledger. Existing integration has no component-ledger hook: implementing one or an equivalent accepted-stage record is part of this slice, not an optional later report.

State is still extensive mol/J, but initialization explicitly adds mechanical energies. Mechanical energies are deterministic functions of current F, so no extra integrated history variable is needed for this reversible-plus-rate-viscous slice. Free sintering/plasticity needs internal variables and a dissipative evolution law; it cannot be represented by changing the reference stretch after each step.

## Independent verification registered before implementation

Manufactured parameter values must be frozen in a separate oracle with units before source implementation; none are sludge defaults. A particularly discriminating analytic path is isochoric diagonal distortion `F=diag(exp(a),exp(-a/2),exp(-a/2))`, so `J=1` and pore volume is constant. For `a=b*t`, fixed composition, constant manufactured total thermal capacity `C [J/K]`, closed heat/fluid boundaries:

`Eelastic=(3/2)*G*V0*b^2*t^2`,
`Einterface=gamma*Aint0*exp(-b*t)`,
`D=(3/2)*eta*V0*b^2`,
`T(t)=T0 + (3/2)*eta*V0*b^2*t/C`,
`Etotal(t)-Etotal(0)=Eelastic(t)-Eelastic(0)+Einterface(t)-Einterface(0)+D*t`.

This verifies nonzero skeleton/interface work with **zero pV work**, and therefore catches substituting gas pressure times bulk volume rate. The host must match separately integrated component work, total U, T and unchanged phase inventories. For a C1 analytic test motion instead of constant rate, take `a=b*(3q^2-2q^3)` and replace `b^2*t` by the analytic integral `integral(a_dot^2 dt)=b^2/L*(12q^3-18q^4+36q^5/5)` on a segment of length L. No endpoint velocity jump is needed. This logarithmic-strain oracle requires an explicitly manufactured analytic motion adapter; the existing provider interpolates stretches, so it cannot be silently treated as interpolating log stretches. Keep that distinction in the oracle identity or derive a separate exact reference for its actual stretch path.

Pre-register three halved steps and absolute acceptance gates before executing: energy/each component-work prefix <=1e-6 J; inventory drift <=1e-12 mol; analytic T error <=1e-6 K **and** compatible with reported inverse error. Choose fixture scales and property-error bounds that make these gates meaningful; do not change gates after observing results. Require error reduction consistent with the actual method until its documented roundoff floor, not an invented all-cases exact order certificate.

A second wet check uses the same isochoric path, eta=0, actual water plus solid/gas caloric providers, closed boundaries and explicitly fixed liquid/gas inventories with the phase-transfer operator not enabled: thermal energy and T remain unchanged even when mechanical energy changes; keep T/P in their supported stable-liquid domain. This is a declared fixed-composition verification limit, not a hidden change to a physical transfer coefficient and not a claim about the general wet process. With active evaporation/condensation, constant thermal energy does not imply constant T because liquid/gas inventories change; that separate check must retain the actual phase-transfer rate, full energy balance and resulting temperature response without imposing the constant-T oracle. This is an integration check of real EOS coupling, not an independent validation of water. A compression path separately verifies changing pore volume and pressure with complete Wtot and an independent energy balance. No EOS run was performed for this design.

Two cells must use one shared gas/liquid/enthalpy/heat face value, cancel it globally at every accepted prefix, preserve fixed solid inventory, and retain all mechanical/body components. Zero motion and an explicitly reversible provider return the original thermal evolution after the constant energy offset; exact input geometry versus admitted 2 ULP geometry must retain the existing distinction. Reject lost positive pore-volume guarantee, out-of-domain mechanical potentials, inconsistent identities, unaccounted solid reactions, nonfinite derivative/error budgets, unsupported capillary/closed-pore inputs, and a caller supplying incompatible pressure-only traction.

## Source audit: three primary sources, equation support only

1. Existing official MOOSE PorousFlow source snapshot `data/sandbox/transport/moose-governing-equations.md`, SHA256 **3215ecc012b321062a2470f032ec480a2569fe3511146adc81110ecc512fa0c8**, independently rehashed this turn. Provenance/license in `data/sandbox/transport/equation_sources.json` and retained MOOSE notices. Actual sections read: Heat flux, lines 233–246; Solid mechanics, lines 247–319. It distinguishes total/effective stress and pore pressure and uses fluid enthalpy in advective energy flux. Its small-strain elastic relation and alternative multiphase pressure measures do not establish our finite-strain constitutive parameters or approve common-pressure wet bricks. [Official governing equations](https://mooseframework.inl.gov/releases/moose/2024-11-11/modules/porous_flow/governing_equations.html).
2. Official MOOSE `StrainEnergyDensity`, live page read 2026-09-07, Description and Monotonic Loading Only sections. It discusses stress-strain work and explicitly limits its implementation for cyclic loading. We therefore do not treat generic accumulated stress work as recoverable elastic energy; our fixture uses an explicit conservative potential and separate dissipation. The official page is equation-context support only; no coefficients were imported and no local full-page snapshot is claimed. [Official material documentation](https://mooseframework.inl.gov/source/materials/StrainEnergyDensity.html).
3. Yang et al., *A thermodynamically consistent phase-field model for viscous sintering*, author-hosted PDF on Purdue, read 2026-09-07, PDF pp. 2–4, equations (1)–(9). It connects an isothermal free-energy functional, surface force and viscous dissipation; the stated omission of relaxation dissipation from its simplified energy law prevents treating that law as universal. Its benchmark concerns viscous particle coalescence, not sludge-brick thermo-poro-mechanics. No viscosity, surface tension or fitted kinetics are transferred. Our lumped diagonal law is our own declared manufactured model, not an implementation of that phase-field paper. [Author-hosted primary paper](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~shen7/pub/sintering.pdf). Public access supports this read; redistribution rights were not inferred, so no full PDF is added to Git.

These sources constrain the energy architecture, not material admission. Real sintering still needs a measured/admitted free-energy and dissipation model, temperature/composition dependence, microstructural state and interface/porosity evolution, mechanical boundary conditions, and independent experiments. Areias total one-direction strain supplies none of those automatically, and cannot set transverse stretch or separate thermal from irreversible shrinkage.
