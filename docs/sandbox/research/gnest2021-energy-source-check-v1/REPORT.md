# Four new source entrances: calorimetry found, complete reaction heat not identified

Two original full texts were obtained and read at relevant sections. They provide measurable quantities, but neither establishes a compatible, mineral-inclusive reaction heat for the GNEST2021 batch. No old retort arithmetic or production model was rerun.

1. **Mendoza Geney2016 doctoral thesis**, [UNAL original repository](https://repositorio.unal.edu.co/handle/unal/58938), title-page year2016 despite file name91511684.2017.pdf. The repository's actual ORIGINAL bitstream was downloaded; its hash and URL are in sources.json. Table8-1, printed189/PDF206, reports DSC measurements of feed and five HRN chars over−10 to50°C. The six Cp labels were visually verified and retained as printed. The same paragraph selects constant1.52kJ/kg/K for its high-temperature model: this extrapolation is a modeling assumption, not high-temperature DSC or measured reaction heat. Uncertainty and the explicit moisture normalization of those DSC specimens remain unknown here. Section3.1.3(printed21/PDF38) describes feed oxygen-bomb calorimetry. Section8.3(printed188/PDF205) uses liquid-by-difference and predetermined final char/water fractions, so its formal model is not an independently stoichiometrically closed reaction mechanism. AppendixB(printed212 onward/PDF229 onward) newly supplies HRN char, gas/tar, mass/energy and CHNO tables. Their existence and headings were read; they were not fully digitized this turn. CHNO bookkeeping is not a complete mineral inventory. No explicit GNEST2021 same-batch certificate was established.

2. **Sánchez Ramírez, Mendoza Geney and Rincón Prat**, *Pirólisis de biosólidos en un horno rotatorio a escala piloto: influencia de la temperatura*, [DOI10.18273/revion.v38n3-2025006](https://doi.org/10.18273/revion.v38n3-2025006). Issue imprint2025,38(3),75–97; publisher online date2026-05-13. Printed84/PDF10 states that char HHV is measured in triplicate using an IKA C2000 BasicS1 isoperibolic bomb under ASTM D240-14, oxygen30bar. Figure7(printed88/PDF14), visually read, labels dry char HHV at250/550/700°C as14118/8614/8194kJ/kg; feed13110 is reported, with earlier characterization cited on printed79/PDF5, and is not relabeled as an independent new feed measurement. The same method explicitly says liquid HHV could not be experimentally determined. Gas heating values are calculated from composition. Thus the new char calorimetry does not close the heat of all pyrolysis products. Figure errorbars are present but not numerically transcribed and remain unknown. The reported feed dry ash42.19% and ad moisture6.43% differ from the2019 retort record; same plant/project does not certify the same batch or allow parameter merging. Carrier nitrogen/pyrolysis conditions and oxygen-bomb analysis serve different purposes: no kinetic A/E is transferred between them.

3. **EUBCE2017**, [official paper entrance](https://www.etaflorence.it/proceedings/?detail=14324), DOI10.5071/25thEUBCE2017-2BO.6.3, pp439–442. The actual page advertises a free PDF. Submitting that exact free-paper form once returns a login/register page. No account was created, access gate bypassed or author contacted. The full text was not read; abstract numbers are not data in this package.

4. **CEST2019**, *Mass and energy balances of sewage sludge pyrolysis in a lab rotary kiln*. The search index points to the [original conference PDF address](https://cms.gnest.org/sites/default/files/cest2019_00857_oral_paper.pdf), but actual web opening returned404 and direct publisher fetch returned403. Search-result title/body mismatch was also observed. No full-text or numerical measurement claim is made from the cached snippet. This is a concrete access outcome, not proof that no copy exists.

The new records distinguish measured low-temperature Cp, measured char combustion HHV, referenced feed HHV, and missing liquid/reaction heat. check_units.py performs only exact kJ↔J/MJ conversions with quantity/basis/label and false-qualification checks. It does not interpret these measurements as thermodynamic formation enthalpies, extrapolate Cp into pyrolysis temperatures, or fit kinetics. Original values, unread errorbars and uncertainty remain separate.

The useful next step is a separate thesis-HRN dataset: inspect AppendixB's printed products and corresponding methods for measured versus assumed liquid/water/gas accounting and mineral exclusions, keeping those experiments separate from GNEST and retort. An independent reaction-energy closure still needs compatible feed/all-product assays, recoveries and reference conditions; direct reaction calorimetry or complete Hess-law inputs cannot be inferred from the existence of a bomb or DSC instrument. No material, training or full-firing qualification is granted.
