# Retort basis audit: printed conversions are usable, raw closure remains unidentified

This audit uses only the already lawfully cached Rincón/Mendoza/Gómez2019 El Salitre monograph, DOI10.19211/KUP9783862199815, and its separate retort experiment. Source hashes and exact print/PDF page locators accompany facts.json. No external source was needed or downloaded, and no GNEST batch identity is assumed.

The method (printed64–66) weighs solid, collects liquid from the vessel/connections/filter, measures liquid water by xylene distillation and collects gas by water displacement with composition/density characterization. Printed71 assigns a 5.17% daf balance discrepancy to tar. These are real recovery procedures, but the section does not supply original collection weights, a signed correction equation or recovery covariance. The table is therefore an adjusted summary; neither exact table closure nor basis conversion restores those missing observations.

Table5-1 uses ar moisture66.70%, ad moisture9.90% and dry ash51.30%. The actual retort charge in section4.2.1 is34.32g at2.8% moisture, after additional drying. In particular, multiplying the table's ad column by34.32g would mix denominators. From the printed charge alone the exact dry mass is33.35904g and initial water0.96096g. These are arithmetic from rounded inputs, not more precise measurements.

For a non-moisture product, y_ad=y_d(1−M_ad/100), y_ar=y_d(1−M_ar/100), and the separately ash-excluded char/gas/tar/generated-water rows obey y_d=y_daf(1−A_d/100). Ash and initial moisture have their own rows and must not be processed through the same daf transform. The checker performs12 exact product/basis conversions and preserves their signed deviations from independently printed targets. All12 have overlapping marginal intervals if every two-decimal input is assumed rounded to nearest±0.005 percentage points. That assumption is expressly conditional; the source does not certify this rounding convention, experimental uncertainty or simultaneous correlated feasibility.

A useful example is gas: directly converting printed8.80%d with9.90% moisture gives7.9288%ad, whereas the table prints7.92. Converting the printed18.06%daf through51.30% ash gives8.79522%d and then7.92449322%ad. These show why chaining rounded published numbers need not reproduce a different printed column. The checker leaves every printed value unchanged; the ad column sum stays99.99%, all others100%.

The reported5.17%daf adjustment has a nominal magnitude2.51779 percentage points on the dry-feed basis. If it is a positive addition to tar, conditional subtraction gives45.18%daf unadjusted tar. This is explicitly an if-then arithmetic diagnostic, not a recovered raw measurement: no signed original equation, raw tar yield or complete recovery ledger is supplied. It must not become a calibration target or be used to claim independently measured100% recovery.

The table of contents ends with conclusions and bibliography, with no retort data appendix listed. A targeted full-text scan for5.17, recovery, appendix/annex, uncertainty and mass balance found the stated adjustment and an unrelated cited report's Annex20, but no raw-retort supplement. Chapter3.4 provides selected heavy metals, not all mineral-forming atoms/mineral oxygen. Product O/S are apportioned from difference under assumed ratios, as already recorded in the follow-up audit. Chapter5.5(printed92–94) scales Table5-1 to a1t/h,70%-moisture example and obtains energy requirements from the heating-value analysis; it is not independent reaction calorimetry. The claim is limited to these actual inspected sections/searches, not that no other records exist anywhere.

Next usable work is a source-specific adjusted-retort accounting dataset retaining raw/adjusted/basis labels and these explicit unknowns. A full material reaction model still needs unadjusted collection results, signed loss allocation, mineral-inclusive assays and compatible independent heat evidence. No reaction parameter, thermodynamic reference, production registry or prior source was changed. Original manuscript/text/images remain private; only factual records and this original analysis are distributed.
