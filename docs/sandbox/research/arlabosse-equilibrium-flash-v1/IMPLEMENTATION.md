## API and numerical meaning

`flash(storage, chemical, total_water_mol, carrier_mol, target_energy_j, policy)` accepts the exact actual `LowMoistureSorptionStorage` and `WaterChemicalPotential` classes. Carrier order is `(O2, N2)` with nonnegative components and positive total. Original Nt may be an exact `Fraction` sum of represented Nc/Nv; target U and carrier values must remain exactly representable binary64. State creation uses the original storage identity and complete U, including the dry excess offset. No K, latent heat source, Q/Hout, or volume work is added.

`FlashPolicy` requires explicit original `InversePolicy`, T subdomain within 325–338 K, inward pressure reserve, composition/inventory/chemical/peq tolerances and finite iteration/call/time budgets. Per T, source liquid TP at inward pressure boundaries prefilters the allowed composition interval. Declared volume and liquid-volume errors enter this prefilter; it is conditional on the existing stable-liquid branch, not a new global EOS proof. Every actual state then passes original full pressure/source/domain checks. All-gas states outside the prefilter are never decoded. Zero total water uses source storage with finite dry excess, named absence of water μ, and no hypothetical finite log(0).

Inner/outer bracket secants use every-third-step midpoint fallback. Near a residual threshold, two actual side probes can establish a small nominal bracket. Encountered domain holes, missing signs, unresolved energy signs, projection loss, limits and exceptions fail with a name. Failure has completed `trials`, counters, cause, plus the same `last_completed_provider_result` object and `(kind, args)` context; the object is saved before a post-callback deadline check. The internal clock cannot interrupt a running provider; the parent owns the external deadline. Finite probes cannot certify absence of unvisited domain holes or global uniqueness.

`NominalEquilibriumCandidate` retains state/point, exact requested Nc+Nv=Nt, **separate signed projection errors** for each inventory and their sum, full U residual, nominal T/chemical composition brackets, peq/pv/μ residuals, source IDs and actual call counters. Its name/qualification explicitly excludes certified inverse status. `certified_temperature_error_bound_k`, `equilibrium_composition_energy_error_j`, and nominal chemical numerical error are `None`. The original energy tolerance and nominal T-width tolerance are enforced, but no common-F or equilibrium-composition error certificate is asserted. Material/training flags are false. Policy definition marks instantaneous equilibrium as a design choice with unknown timescale error; background modeling precedents are not inserted into actual caloric/pressure source IDs.
