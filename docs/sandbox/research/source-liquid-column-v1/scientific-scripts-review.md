check_liquid.py builds an explicitly artificial-liquid test. Its reference RHS independently computes printed dry Cp energy, aggregate temperature inverse with brentq, artificial liquid volume/pressure, nonconstant saturation mobility, series Darcy resistance, donor flow and h=u+pv. DOP853 reference does not call production source-column/storage inverse, wet face/phase wrappers or liquid transport helpers. It does reuse gas caloric functions, water chemical equilibrium and gas face primitive. The implementation report states this limited independence accurately. Production integration is called only for the compared2/4/8-step trajectories, not to generate its reference. Saved differences and refinement ratios are calculated from actual output arrays versus the DOP853 endpoint; reported tables match stored results. These support smooth manufactured assembly/time-refinement behavior, not independent constitutive or material validation.

native-review/audit_saved.py imports only pathlib, fractions, json, hashlib and math. It reads saved outputs, reconstructs exact Darcy Q/J/H from recorded geometry and source-labelled liquid v/h, then checks represented outputs, donor/projection/direction error, midpoint integrals and local/global balances. It does not import models, execute EOS, or fabricate new native states. Saved donor v/h are accepted primitives rather than independently verified thermodynamics; report says so explicitly. The166 named comparisons are actual check(...) expressions with assert, not a hardcoded success count or runner count reused as evidence. Material/direction qualifiers and complete one-step/three-stage gates are checked. Stage rate table in the report matches saved audit rows.

Global balance residual strings are written as0 only after exact equality assertions. For the fixed closed-left/open-right test, the omitted left external term is zero by the constructed model and saved local/global equality checks. Negative molar flow with positive enthalpy flow is consistent with the declared negative water enthalpy reference. No separate latent heat or duplicate liquid energy term appears in these audits.
