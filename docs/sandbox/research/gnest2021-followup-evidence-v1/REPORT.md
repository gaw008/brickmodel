# El Salitre follow-up: direct recovery methods found, full chemical closure remains open

GNEST2021 reference 45 leads to Rincón, Mendoza and Gómez's 2019 research monograph, *Tratamiento térmico de biosólidos para aplicaciones energéticas: Pirólisis y conversión de sus alquitranes*, DOI [10.19211/KUP9783862199815](https://doi.org/10.19211/KUP9783862199815). The [publisher's public full PDF](https://www.uni-kassel.de/upress/online/OpenAccess/978-3-86219-980-8.OpenAccess.pdf) was downloaded and read locally. This is original experimental reporting from the same authors and El Salitre plant, but the feed collection batch and statistical independence from GNEST are not established. It cannot be merged into GNEST's material identity. Chapter 3 uses dry ash 51.30% at 900°C; GNEST's differing ash/ultimate values reinforce the need to keep records separate.

The useful new experiment is ISO647 retort pyrolysis: 34.32 g at 2.8% moisture, 0.2–1 mm particles, final 520°C after 80 minutes (section4.2.1, printed61/PDF70). Section4.3.1 (printed64–66/PDF73–75) describes actual solid weighing, liquid/container/connection/filter weighing, displaced-water gas collection with Tedlar sampling, and water determination by xylene distillation at 140°C under ASTM D95-05. Gas composition is measured chromatographically and density is also measured by Bunsen–Schilling. This supplies a concrete recovery method absent from GNEST's liquid-by-difference approximation.

However, section5.1 (printed71/PDF80) assigns a 5.17% daf mass-balance discrepancy to tar, the most uncertain recovered product. Published Table5-1 yields are therefore adjusted outputs, not independent raw recovery measurements. It separately lists char and ash, generated water and initial moisture. Table5-3 labels CO2/CO/H2/CH4 **mass** fractions, not mole fractions; measured density 1.28 kg/m³ differs from composition-derived 1.16. No recovery covariance or quantitative sampling-loss uncertainty was established in the read sections. The printed ad total is 99.99%, retained unchanged.

Table5-2 O and S are not direct independent determinations: the method partitions remaining mass using O/(S+O) ratios 0.104 for char and 0.809 for tar, referring to earlier same-lot analyses without publishing their complete raw observations. The method paragraph initially names oxygen among analyzer outputs but immediately explicitly denies direct O/S determination; the latter restriction and section5.1 are retained. Chapter3 oxygen is also by difference. Section3.4 provides selected heavy-metal atomic-absorption evidence, not a complete inventory of ash-forming elements and mineral oxygen. Thus the source cannot close GNEST's mineral-inclusive H/O accounting or validate its TG3 balance by parameter substitution.

There is direct **feed combustion** calorimetry: section3.3 and Table3-4 (printed47–49/PDF56–58) report an IKA oxygen bomb and dry HHV 11.30 MJ/kg. LHV 10.51 MJ/kg is derived; the printed hydrogen and Eq3-1 give 10.50944. This distinction matters because 11.30 must not be relabelled as LHV or transferred to GNEST. Product calorific values are derived from the Mason–Gandhi correlation and conversions, and the energetic process comparison uses those values. Neither feed combustion calorimetry nor the product heating-value balance is direct pyrolysis reaction calorimetry or time-resolved reaction enthalpy.

Tables5-1,5-2,5-3 were visually checked on actual PDF pages81–83, then transcribed to the new quantitative record. The arithmetic checker verifies their printed row sets and column sums, not experimental accuracy. No original PDF, page image or extracted full text is redistributed; the publisher imprint states copyright and no explicit reuse license was established.

A second bounded candidate was reference61: Mendoza, Gómez and Rincón, *Catalytic cracking of tars produced during sewage sludge pyrolysis over a fixed bed of sewage sludge char*, 23rd European Biomass Conference, Vienna, June2015 (printed117/PDF126). The monograph's printed70/PDF79 refers to same-origin tar information, not a cross-publication same-batch certificate. Exact-title and conference-domain searches did not yield a lawful primary full text in this run. A request-full-text listing is not numerical evidence and no author was contacted. No numbers were imported from that candidate and no third candidate was pursued.

The executable next step is to audit this retort record as its own material experiment: retain the 5.17% daf allocation as a separate correction, establish whether unadjusted recovery and assay records are publicly available, and quantify only the basis transformations supported by the printed rows. A complete reaction-energy fit still requires independent product recovery/composition (including mineral atoms) and compatible enthalpy evidence. This follow-up reduces the missing-method and quantitative-endpoint gap; it does not admit a full raw-sludge reaction model, brick firing calibration or material validation.
