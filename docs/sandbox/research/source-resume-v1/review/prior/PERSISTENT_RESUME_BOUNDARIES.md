Offline time has a specific transition: successful save-and-suspend has completed and the old live session is closed. Time in an ordinary in-process pause remains charged as before. A wall-clock reversal makes D unavailable/clock_discontinuity; it does not erase S or fail the solver solely because of the date. The primitive clock values and already-measured aggregate debit must be retained, without inventing retrospective sub-timings.

The source-session budget and the comparison worker's combined budget are distinct. If a fresh continuous comparison branch has already run, its actual work must remain charged in the worker-level lineage when another process resumes the paused branch. A parent+pause+restore lineage adds four rebuild providers; a separate continuous comparison adds another four. Do not reuse the preceding stage's 12-constructor total as the expected total for a new two-process comparison.

Minimum concrete acceptance cases for the implementation review:

1. A real source-class manufactured nonstationary prefix: continuous and separate-process pause/restore have identical original and new states, times, complete Rates observations, steps and full-chain balances. One original eight-callback pause resumes with fourteen new callbacks in the fixed three-step case; the new four constructors are separately observed.
2. Rehash after deleting a failed/rejected capture, swapping same-time captures, changing cp Rates or shrinking counts: reject before native construction. Ordinary checkpoint replay alone is insufficient.
3. Original step/rejection/wall/callback budget near exhaustion, plus actual reconstruction debit: reject under the same original limit. A repeated failed or unfinished claim cannot grant another allowance.
4. Two concurrent restore attempts; second constructor failure; returned constructor followed by journal failure; process killed after claim; final save publication failure. Preserve actual starts/returns and original exceptions, and leave no resumable old claim.
5. Completed save closes the old live session. An ordinary online pause keeps accumulating cost; an offline date gap does not. Clock reversal affects only D diagnostics. Check S+A with a virtual clock, including slow finalization.

The record establishes consistency of retained local evidence and same-version reconstruction. Hashes, passively replayed Rates and historical source IDs do not authenticate arbitrary user-rewritten archives or independently prove past EOS calls. Retain the original study's no-resume/no-material flags; the new, narrowly validated source-trajectory record owns its separate operational resume permission.
