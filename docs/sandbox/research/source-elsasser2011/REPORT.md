# Elsässer 2011: newly retrieved primary calorimetry evidence

University record: https://dspace.vut.cz/items/23fc5dfb-6d75-43d8-bb33-b28bb2586ef8

The actual PDF was downloaded and pages 41–48 and Annex II (117) read locally.
PDF hash, retrieval locator, sample conditions and 14 transcribed values are in
`cp_evidence.json`. Annex II was also rendered and visually checked. Public
availability is not a redistribution license; the complete PDF is not committed.

The dried sample retains 8.1% water. The dry-solid Cp values are derived by
subtracting water's contribution, not direct calorimetry of water-free material.
The experiment is near ambient temperature. Annex II labels ±0.0360 as the
95% confidence interval, whereas its standard deviation is about 0.0628
kJ/(kg K). These quantities must not be interchanged.

Independent Decimal recomputation of the 14 printed values gives mean
0.89902643 and sample SD 0.06281454 kJ/(kg K). The printed SD is 0.062813:
retain this small rounding-level discrepancy, do not overwrite the source.

Admission remains conditional: no high-temperature Cp curve, matching kinetics,
complete composition or brick-scale applicability follows from this measurement.
The current manufactured material is unchanged. Repeated calorimetry on this
sample is not 14 independent materials or held-out external validation.
