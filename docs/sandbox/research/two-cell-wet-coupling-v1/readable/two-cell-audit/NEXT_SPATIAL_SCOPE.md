# Fixed-domain 2-to-4-cell extension

Read-only design from the frozen two-cell fixture and current reacting_skeleton_energy.py, skeleton_energy.py, SolidReactionConfig and prescribed geometry. No EOS, tests, code edits or new native run. This blueprint does not upgrade P01/P16 or the raw-material/free-sintering Goal.

## Scope and fixed domain

Keep half_thickness=.02 m, reference area=.01 m2 and the SAME physical interval, manufactured transport coefficients and external sealed boundaries. Change cells from 2 to 4: reference widths .01 -> .005 m, reference cell volumes 1e-4 -> 5e-5 m3. Every face area remains .01 m2 before deformation; it is not divided by two. Normal and tangential smoothstep stretches remain the same function of time; four normal entries repeat the same spatially uniform prescribed stretch. At every time each parent's width/volume and volume-rate equals the sum of its two children, and global boundary position/face area remain unchanged.

The smallest useful native comparison is coupled transport at two spatial grids with time refinement separately controlled. Existing coupled 2-cell coarse/fine time runs can be reused only if exact source/physical inputs agree with the new preregistration. Run the 4-cell version at the corresponding two time caps, sequentially and with fixed declared budgets. Do not conflate changing spatial cells with changing total physical slab thickness, reaction parameters or local phase coefficient density.

## Extensive scaling is required, especially q(N)

Current skeleton is q(N)=q0+sum(w_i*N_i) multiplied by reference elastic/interface energies, stresses and viscous response. Keeping w_B=10 per mol while halving each inventory changes q and therefore the modeled material. The current API can express mesh-consistent coefficients without new source code, but the fixture must construct them from intensive constants.

Fix beta_B=w_B*Vref=10*1e-4=.001 m3/mol and beta_A=0. Use q=1+beta_B*N_B/Vref. Thus each 4-cell weight is w_B=20 per mol, w_A=0, q0 remains 1. Initial N_B=0 makes naive scaling look harmless at t0; the error appears as soon as reaction produces B, which is why initial-energy checks alone do not verify it.

Keep bulk modulus1000 Pa, shear modulus400 Pa, viscosity3 Pa s and gamma100 J/m2 unchanged. The reference micro-interface area is an EXTENSIVE assigned internal surface measure: .003 m2 per old cell -> .0015 m2 per child, equivalent area density30 m2/m3. It is not the finite-volume face area and does not acquire another contribution because a computational interface was inserted. Reference elastic/viscous energy already scales with reference volume. With q unchanged and interface area halved, each child's elastic/interface energy, fixed-N deformation power, dissipation and recoverable storage are exactly half in rational input arithmetic, apart from the documented floating implementation enclosure.

Composition derivatives stay consistent: dE_child/dN_B_child = w_child*E_reference_child = (2w_parent)*(E_reference_parent/2). The constitutive pressure/stress and intensive chemical mechanical coupling therefore remain invariant. q0 must not be halved; doing so also changes the constitutive response.

The A/B caloric coefficients, molar volumes and per-mole uncertainty bounds remain unchanged. Set skeleton.fixed_solid_inventory_mol to A=1,B=0 for each child, and all actual inventories to half the parent, including vapor/liquid/carrier. Solid storage bulk volume is5e-5; explicitly derived bulk-volume uncertainty should use the same reference-volume-relative bound (old1e-18 -> child5e-19), not silently double total geometry uncertainty. If an uncertainty is intentionally a per-cell numerical floor instead, record its different aggregation separately and do not call it preserved physical uncertainty.

## Phase and reaction extensivity

SolidReactionConfig already uses concentrations per CURRENT bulk volume. With halved N and bulk volume, concentrations and T are identical, so the same network/prefactor/Ea gives each child's species source half the parent's. Keep kinetics unchanged; do not halve k=.1/s.

WaterPhaseTransfer's coefficient has units mol/s/Pa and is a lumped PER-CELL coefficient, not a coefficient per volume. To preserve the original physical volumetric rate law, fix coefficient/Vref=1e-6/1e-4=.01 mol/(s Pa m3). Use5e-7 mol/s/Pa per child. Keeping1e-6 per new cell doubles the slab's evaporation conductance. This is an explicit manufactured volumetric interpretation of the original coefficient, not newly sourced phase kinetics. Check it in an isothermal uniform refinement test.

Gas thermal conductivity, diffusivity, permeability, relative permeability and viscosity remain intensive and unchanged. Current face distances are generated by the refined geometry; serial transport coefficients must use them normally. Do not scale diffusivity or conductivity to force equal discrete initial face fluxes.

## Initial nonuniform profile and exact conservative prolongation

Use the same piecewise-constant continuum initial condition as the 2-cell case: left half has T=300 K and its declared densities; right half T=301 K with its different vapor density. The 4-cell child temperatures are [300,300,301,301] and inventory rows are parent/2, assigned [left/2,left/2,right/2,right/2]. Do not linearly interpolate four new temperature values while claiming the physical initial field is unchanged.

For every species, child amounts obtained by binary division by two preserve the parent sum exactly when representable. Require exact Fraction equality for each parent pair and globally, checking no underflow. For energy, there are two separate checks:

1. Build the fine host from the correctly scaled physical model and call state_from_temperatures at the inherited temperatures. This independently checks that the extensive storage law predicts child energy near parent/2 under the source/numerical error enclosure. Record actual discrepancies; do not use a relaxed event tolerance as an unexplained initial-energy adjustment.
2. If exact initial total E is required for the mesh comparison, use explicitly CONSERVATIVE PROLONGATION: assign child represented total E=parent_E/2 (verified finite/representable), tagged with the fine host's actual energy identity, and decode through the fine host. Require each child temperature agrees with its inherited parent temperature within the summed inverse/source/reconstruction bounds. The input thermodynamic state is then defined by N/E; the nominal initial temperatures are qualified decoded values. This is not copying arbitrary energy into an incompatible model because the extensive-law check in step1 establishes the correspondence. Preserve both the temperature-built and conservatively prolonged state evidence.

If these checks do not agree within the declared enclosures, reject the refinement setup and fix scaling/identity. Do not enforce both an exact nominal T and exact total E by silently changing caloric values. The conservative N/E option avoids a spurious initial energy difference being mistaken for spatial truncation error.

## Independent verification before native trajectories

A manufactured no-EOS extensive scaling test should compare one parent against two identical children for: solid and gas volumes, phase-transfer coefficient, reaction source, all skeleton energies/powers, q, stresses and dE/dN. Check a nonzero B inventory as well as B=0. Only identity fields and cell indices should differ; the corresponding physical densities must agree.

A uniform-profile zero-gradient case must have zero internal transport on either grid and reproduce parent-pair extensive histories within existing temporal/roundoff criteria. This separates assembly/scaling mistakes from spatial approximation error. For actual two-cell gradients, retain the independent mixture-diffusion correction and face-temperature enthalpy oracle; no per-cell carrier invariant may be assumed.

Compare total N/E and parent-pair sums of the 4-cell solution against corresponding 2-cell inventories/energies at the same time. These are conservative quantities. For temperature, either compare a declared reference-volume-weighted diagnostic average of child temperatures, labelled an average rather than a thermodynamic temperature, or decode the aggregated child N/E with the parent storage model and the parent's current geometry to obtain a consistent coarse-scale thermodynamic temperature. Do not average pressures or temperatures and call that conservation.

Every grid retains local signed face balances and exact global internal-face cancellation, source identity, closed external boundaries, total water/carrier/A+B and named energy work. Source uncertainty aggregation uses extensive sums; per-cell inverse numerical floors may accumulate differently and must be included in comparison indicators. The existing tolerance scales are local step-control indicators, not proven global errors; keep time-cap differences separate from spatial-grid differences.

## Interpretation of the discontinuous initial profile

At the central jump, the coarse center distance is .01 m and the fine center distance .005 m. Fourier and concentration-gradient flux at the first instant generally double on the refined grid. That is expected for a discontinuous piecewise initial field, not evidence of a scaling error and not something to remove by rescaling coefficients. The .00001526 s pilot may be far shorter than the thermal/diffusive time over a .005 m cell, so the jump remains severely unresolved. A 2-to-4 discrepancy can therefore be informative without being small.

Do not call two spatial samples convergence. Report the signed conservative grid discrepancy and each grid's time-cap discrepancy, and leave mesh-resolution qualification unresolved. A later 8-cell case and/or a separately registered smooth initial field would be needed to investigate an asymptotic convergence rate. Changing to a smooth field is a new physical initial-value problem; its coarse states should be conservative cell averages, not center samples with different total N/E.
