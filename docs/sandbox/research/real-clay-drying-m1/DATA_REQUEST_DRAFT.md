Subject: Clarification and existing data for your green-brick drying experiments

Dear Dr. Mancuhan,

I am developing a source-traceable physics model of green-brick drying and have read your 2016 paper, “Experimental investigation of green brick shrinkage behavior with Bigot’s curves,” and Selin Özen’s 2014 thesis.

Before using the results for calibration and separate validation, could you help clarify three points?

1. Which records belong to the same experiments? The thesis and article report different air velocities. The thesis also describes the temperature sensor both at the surface and at mid-thickness. What was the actual sensor location and specimen geometry for the relevant curves?
2. Are existing time series of moisture or mass, sample temperature, and drying-air temperature/humidity available for one clay mixture, preferably the 40% clay mixture, across the reported drying conditions? A published table or existing data export would be sufficient; no new experiment is requested. The corresponding initial/dry mass definitions and any available measurement uncertainty would be helpful.
3. Are any existing thermal or moisture-property measurements available for that mixture? This is optional; it would help us distinguish measured properties from parameters that must be estimated.

Please also let me know any conditions for reuse and attribution. I will keep the thesis and article records separate unless their relationship is confirmed.

Thank you for your time.

## 为什么问这些

第一项确定预测的温度到底对应哪里，并避免错误合并记录；第二项限定实际边界及误差评价，并提供校准/验证分工依据。数值表是便利形式，不把拿不到电子原日志当成普遍否决条件。第三项为可选，避免把作者没有的数据列为强制要求。收到回复不自动等于A通过，仍按具体内容决定。
