Description: This collection contains 10 dataset. Description of each dataset is found in the Metadata.

The collection contains the following datasets:
1) X-ray diffraction analysis of clay minerals for brick clay, danish yellow and red brick clay
2) X-ray diffraction bulk analysis of brick clay and sewage sludge ash
3) X-ray diffraction bulk analysis of fired brick discs with brick clay and sewage sludge ash
4) Leaching from fired brick discs with brick clay and sewage sludge ash
5) Brick discs with sewage sludge ash: Production data, water absorption, porosity and density measurements
6) Thermogravimetric analysis (TGA) of brick clay and sewage sludge ash
7) Particle size distribution of sewage sludge ash
8) Leaching of heavy metals from brick clay and sewage sludge ash, measured with ICP-OES and ICP-MS
9) Scanning electron microscopy of fired discs with brick clay and sewage sludge ash
10) X-ray fluorescence (XRF) - Chemical characterisation of brick clay and SSA

Explanation of variables:
SSA: Sewage sludge ash
SSA-P1-Raw: SSA from Avedøre wastewater treatment plant, no treatment
SSA-P1-ED: SSA from Avedøre wastewater treatment plant, treated with electrodialytic separation
SSA-P2-Raw: SSA from Lynetten wastewater treatment plant, no treatment
SSA-P2-ED: SSA from Lynetten wastewater treatment plant, treated with electrodialytic separation
SSA-P2-A2P: SSA from Lynetten wastewater treatment plant, treated with Ash2Phos
Y: Brick clay with high carbonate content, from Egernsund Wienerberger, used for yellow fired bricks
R: Brick clay with low carbonate content, from Egernsund Wienerberger, used for red fired bricks

Y-Ref: Brick disc with yellow brick clay
Y-P1-Raw: Brick disc with yellow brick clay and 30% SSA-P1-Raw
Y-P1-ED: Brick disc with yellow brick clay and 30% SSA-P1-ED
Y-P2-Raw:  Brick disc with yellow brick clay and 30% SSA-P2-Raw
Y-P2-ED:  Brick disc with yellow brick clay and 30% SSA-P2-ED
Y-P2-A2P:  Brick disc with yellow brick clay and 30% SSA-P2-A2P

R-Ref: Brick disc with red brick clay
R-P1-Raw: Brick disc with red brick clay and 30% SSA-P1-Raw
R-P1-ED: Brick disc with red brick clay and 30% SSA-P1-ED
R-P2-Raw:  Brick disc with red brick clay and 30% SSA-P2-Raw
R-P2-ED:  Brick disc with red brick clay and 30% SSA-P2-ED
R-P2-A2P:  Brick disc with red brick clay and 30% SSA-P2-A2P
